Who we are today

Rooted in community. Focused on sustainable development.

The Asaba Memorial Education Foundation (AMEF) is a registered Community-Based Organisation (CBO) in Uganda, operating under Registration No. CBS/293/2026. AMEF is committed to empowering communities in Kiruli Sub-County and Masindi District through holistic and sustainable development initiatives.

The Foundation focuses on education, vocational skills development, community empowerment, HIV/AIDS awareness, and child protection. Through these interventions, AMEF seeks to improve access to opportunities, strengthen community capacity, protect vulnerable children, and promote greater awareness of health and social issues affecting communities.

AMEF’s overall goal is to contribute to the development of stronger, healthier, skilled, and self-reliant communities by working closely with community members, local leaders, institutions, and other development partners. The organisation believes that sustainable community transformation is achieved by equipping people with knowledge, practical skills, opportunities, and the capacity to actively participate in addressing challenges within their communities.

Our history

A History We Must Never Forget

The Asaba Memorial Education Foundation (AMEF) was founded by Bienvenu Byamungu with a vision to give hope to young people who had lost hope of accessing secondary education. Having witnessed the challenges faced by children in underserved communities, he believed that every child deserves an opportunity to learn, develop their potential, and build a better future regardless of their background.

AMEF was established in memory of Abwooli Asaba Mary, who passed away on 21 March 2022 after suffering from diabetes for so many years. Her memory continues to inspire the Foundation’s commitment to education, empowerment, health, improved livelihoods, and sustainable community development.

One history we must never forget is that the entire Kiruli Sub-county had never had a secondary school until AMEF established Asaba Memorial High School (AMHS) in February 2026. For many years, children who completed primary education had to travel long distances to access secondary education. Many families could not afford to send their children far from home, contributing to a high rate of school dropout, particularly among girls and vulnerable children. Child marriage, HIV/AIDS, unemployment, and poverty are also serious challenges affecting the community and limiting opportunities for young people and families.

AMEF established AMHS to change this reality by bringing quality secondary education closer to the community. The school provides academic education together with practical skills, leadership development, ICT, entrepreneurship, and other opportunities that prepare students for their future.

Beyond education, AMEF is committed to improving the lives of children, youth, and women through skills development, economic empowerment, HIV/AIDS prevention and awareness, access to clean water and sanitation, health initiatives, and improved livelihoods. Through Sifa Skilling Hub (SSH), AMEF provides vocational training to youth and women, equipping them with practical skills, entrepreneurship opportunities, and the knowledge needed to become self-reliant and improve their livelihoods.

The school officially launched on 17 July 2026, marking an important milestone in AMEF’s journey. The Area Member of Parliament for Buruli attended as the Guest of Honour, while Rev. Fr. George Onor of Our Lady of Assumption Kyatiri Catholic Parish led the Holy Mass and prayers, giving thanks to God and blessing the school, its students, staff, and future.

Our story is not only about remembering the past. It is about turning remembrance into action, turning hopelessness into hope, and creating opportunities for generations to come.
