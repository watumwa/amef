Education Programme

Education should bring opportunity closer to home.

AMEF is expanding access to secondary education and practical learning so children and young people can develop the knowledge, confidence and skills they need for a better future.

Why it matters

Education is not a privilege; it is a human right.

The Universal Declaration of Human Rights (Article 26) recognises that everyone has the right to education. Yet, millions of children around the world remain out of school, particularly those living in poor, rural and underserved communities where access to quality education is limited.

Kiruli Sub-County in Masindi District, Uganda, is one such community where children have historically faced barriers to accessing primary and secondary education. Today, this is changing. In February 2026, Asaba Memorial Education Foundation (AMEF) established Asaba Memorial High School (AMHS) in Kitanyata Village, Kiruli Sub-County, Masindi District. Through this AMEF effort, AMHS is currently the only secondary school within the entire Kiruli Sub-County and surrounding communities, bringing secondary education closer to children who have previously been underserved.

For many children, particularly girls, limited access to secondary education can increase the risk of school dropout, early marriage and teenage pregnancy. By bringing secondary education closer to the community, AMEF is working to create greater opportunities for children to remain in school and continue their education.

Through AMHS, AMEF is not only expanding access to secondary education but also creating opportunities for young people to learn, develop practical and vocational skills, discover their potential and become changemakers in their communities.

Our approach

Knowledge, confidence and practical ability.

Vocationalising secondary education

Practical and employable skills alongside academic study.

Through AMHS, AMEF is vocationalising secondary education by giving students opportunities to learn practical and employable skills alongside their academic studies. This approach enables learners to discover their talents, develop practical competencies, solve real-life problems and explore pathways towards employment, entrepreneurship and self-reliance.

Students have opportunities to develop skills in areas such as:

  • Computer and ICT
  • Tailoring and Fashion Design
  • Hairdressing and Hair Cutting
  • Bakery
  • Welding
  • Carpentry
  • Soap and Cosmetic Making
  • Agri-business
  • Entrepreneurship and Leadership

Learning through real-world projects

Learning by doing.

Through Project-Based Learning at AMHS, AMEF encourages students to identify challenges within their school and community, develop ideas, work collaboratively and create practical solutions. Rather than only learning about problems, students are encouraged to learn by doing.

Brass Band Club (BBC)

Discovering talent through music.

AMEF established the Brass Band Club (BBC) at Asaba Memorial High School (AMHS) to give students an opportunity to discover and develop their talents through music.

The club provides training in brass band instruments, music performance, teamwork, discipline and leadership while building confidence and creativity.

School Feeding Programme

Helping vulnerable learners stay in school.

AMEF recognises that hunger and food insecurity can make it difficult for children to attend school regularly, concentrate in class and remain in education.

Our School Feeding Programme supports vulnerable learners, especially girls, orphans and children from low-income families, by providing nutritious meals during the school day.

Co-curricular development

Holistic learning beyond the classroom.

AMEF promotes talent development and holistic education through co-curricular activities that help students discover and develop their abilities, confidence, creativity, teamwork and leadership skills.

Activities include Brass Band, Debates, Spelling Bee, Music, Dance and Drama (MDD), Games and Sports.

International School Exchange Programme

Connecting students in Kiruli with the wider world.

AMEF is a member of the Global Schools Alliance, an alliance that connects schools around the world. The partnership between AMHS and International Book Project was established through AMEF, helping connect AMHS students with students around the world.

Building on these relationships, AMEF seeks to establish further school-to-school exchange partnerships between Asaba Memorial High School and schools in the United States, Canada, Europe, Rwanda, South Africa and other countries.

Featured AMEF programme

Asaba Memorial High School (AMHS)

AMEF education initiative · Established February 2026

Bringing a secondary school close to home.

Before AMEF established Asaba Memorial High School, Kiruli Sub-County had no secondary school. Children who completed primary school had to travel long distances to continue their education, and many families could not bear the cost.

AMEF created AMHS to provide safe, accessible and quality secondary education closer to the community, while combining academic learning with practical skills, leadership and enterprise development.
