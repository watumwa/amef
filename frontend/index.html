AMEF established Asaba Memorial High School in February 2026 to change that reality, combining academic learning with practical skills, leadership, ICT and entrepreneurship.

Our history

A history we must never forget

The Asaba Memorial Education Foundation was founded by Bienvenu Byamungu with a vision to give hope to young people who had lost hope of accessing secondary education. Having witnessed the challenges faced by children in underserved communities, he believed that every child deserves an opportunity to learn, develop their potential and build a better future regardless of their background.

AMEF was established in memory of Abwooli Asaba Mary, who passed away on 21 March 2022 after suffering from diabetes for many years. Her memory continues to inspire the Foundation’s commitment to education, empowerment, health, improved livelihoods and sustainable community development.

Kiruli Sub-county had never had a secondary school until AMEF established Asaba Memorial High School in February 2026. For many years, children who completed primary education had to travel long distances to access secondary education. Many families could not afford to send their children far from home, contributing to school dropout, particularly among girls and vulnerable children.

AMEF established AMHS to change this reality by bringing quality secondary education closer to the community. Beyond education, the Foundation works to improve the lives of children, youth and women through skills development, economic empowerment, HIV/AIDS prevention and awareness, clean water and sanitation, health initiatives and improved livelihoods.

The school officially launched on 17 July 2026. The Area Member of Parliament for Buruli attended as Guest of Honour, while Rev. Fr. George Onor of Our Lady of Assumption Kyatiri Catholic Parish led Holy Mass and prayers, giving thanks and blessing the school, its students, staff and future.

Our story is not only about remembering the past. It is about turning remembrance into action.
