From local challenges to local solutions

Opportunity brought closer to the community.

For years, many children in Kiruli Sub-County had to travel long distances to access secondary education. AMEF responded by establishing Asaba Memorial High School and Sifa Skilling Hub, bringing secondary education, vocational training and practical skills closer to the community.

These initiatives demonstrate what is possible when local commitment is matched with opportunity. With the right partnerships, we can take this work further and reach more communities.
